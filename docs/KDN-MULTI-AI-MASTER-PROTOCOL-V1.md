## Rules
1. Single source of truth: task ledger in GitHub, versioned with commit references.
2. No repeated copy-paste requests: use available connected tools first. Ask the human only for essential access, approvals or device-only actions.
3. Never paste API keys, passwords, tokens, private data, user records, or paid account credentials into a multi-AI broadcast.
4. No AI may approve production deployment, DNS changes, billing, purchases, outreach, or publication on behalf of the human without explicit authorization.
5. Work in review branches; preserve backups, rollback plans and production stability.
6. Distinguish proposed, coded, tested, deployed and verified-live states.
7. Independent reviewer required for security-sensitive changes.
8. Cite primary sources for news, law, government, elections and public representative claims; avoid fabricated sources.
9. MY NETA and governance tools must remain nonpartisan, accessible and transparent; protect personal data.
10. Use Hindi as coordination language; English technical terms allowed. Keep output concise and copy-paste ready.
11. If a room cannot contribute, reply NOT_NEEDED with a one-sentence reason; do not invent work.
12. The human is the relay. Rooms do not share live memory, files or tools merely because they received this protocol.
